# Careers: ranks on top of the short shifts (#261, first slice)

## Why

#261 asks for careers with promotions (cop, firefighter, EMT, taxi, delivery,
DJ, chef, stylist, photographer, real estate, racer). Short shifts (#208,
`shifts.ts`) already count lifetime shifts per job in `save.shifts.worked`,
made as the rank base. This slice is the pure career and rank model, wired to
those shifts and the wallet, with ranks visible in the progression menu. The
other careers each need their own shift mechanic and are follow-up issues.

## Decisions (agent's calls; no owner Q&A was possible, flag in review)

- A **career** groups one or more shift jobs and has five ranks. It is
  derived, not stored: rank = how many of the career's lifetime shifts clear
  `RANK_SHIFTS` (0, 3, 8, 16, 30). So the save does not change at all, old
  saves just have a rank from the shifts they already counted, and the server
  progress work (#272, #366) needs nothing new.
- First careers are the ones whose jobs already exist, so every shift job
  belongs to one: **Kitchen** (ramen, halo-halo; Dishwasher, Prep cook, Line
  cook, Sous chef, Head chef), **Cafe** (barista, boba; Trainee, Counter hand,
  Barista, Shift lead, Cafe manager) and **Zoo keeper** (feeding; Volunteer,
  Feeder, Keeper, Senior keeper, Head keeper). Cafe and Zoo keeper are not in
  the issue's list; they are what the existing stations map to, and the
  issue's chef is Kitchen.
- A promotion is a **raise and a bonus**: each rank adds `RAISE_PER_RANK`
  (10%) to the wage of the shift (rounded down, after slow hours), and the
  shift that reaches a new rank pays `PROMOTION_BONUS` (50) coins times the
  new rank number, through `earn` so the wallet cap holds. Hustle XP is
  unchanged. Slow-hours shifts still count toward rank (they pay half, and the
  three full-pay shifts a day already cap the rate).
- Nothing is locked: ranks only pay more. All jobs here are non-alcohol, so
  all 13+. Cop, firefighter and EMT are not in this slice because they
  touch the crime and emergency loops and the age tiers (see the follow-ups).
- UI: the game menu's "Your climb" gets a Careers group (rank, shifts to the
  next rank, the five ranks with their raise), and the pay slip after a shift
  says when it earned a promotion. No new NPC dialogue in this slice; boss
  lines belong with the follow-up that adds the boss NPCs.

## Follow-ups

Cop, firefighter, EMT, taxi, delivery, DJ, stylist, photographer, real estate
and racer: one issue each, because each needs its own shift or mission loop
before ranks can mean anything. They plug in by adding a `CAREERS` entry and
the counter that feeds it.
