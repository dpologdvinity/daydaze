# Daydaze hair: licensed model catalog

Compiled 2026-11-19 for the owner decision to drop the procedural hair
(`art/doll/blender/doll_hair.py`) and the Capcom-ripped Hair Packs (#314) in favor
of downloaded, licensed hair. Refs #312, #314. Nothing here is fitted into the game yet.

Downloads live in the git-ignored `local-assets/downloads/hair-<source>-<slug>/`
(main checkout). Ratings come from each model's preview image, not from a
fitted render: **good** = looks like believable hair on an adult doll, **okay** =
usable with a fit pass or a retexture, **poor** = toon, crude or far too heavy.
Triangle counts are measured from the downloaded files.

Found about 1,200 Sketchfab search hits (searched about 40 terms, CC-BY and CC0,
downloadable), narrowed to about 740 hair-like by name and tags, of which 72 were
accepted and downloaded. Accepted and downloaded: 148 models in the first pass, 37 more in the second. Rejected: the other
Sketchfab hits (rips, AI-generated, not hair, real-person likenesses) plus 3
MakeHuman assets, listed by pattern below.

**Best fit news:** the 67 MakeHuman proxies are made for our exact base mesh, so
they fit with the existing `proxies.py` with no retopo. The Sketchfab hair needs
a refit like `pack_hair.py` did, and many of the best Sketchfab hair is heavy
(80k to 900k tris) and needs decimation or retopo for the 300k-triangle budget.

**Gaps, honestly (after the second pass):**

- **Twin tails:** now two licensed options. `summer66 Twintail` (CC-BY, 27k tris,
  untextured, high twin tails with curled ends, rated okay) is the real twin tails.
  joan8ied's pigtails are two long braids hanging forward (good, textured), so
  they cover `pigtails` and braided twin styles. `sakae12345 HAIR` stays as the
  short anime pair.
- **Black and textured hair:** afros, curls and coily puffs are covered well
  (MotionStudioArts, continuumreed, Elvaerwyn). Locs, box braids and cornrows
  exist but are heavy or toon. **Still no licensed twist-out, knotless braids,
  crochet braids, senegalese twists, faux locs or silk press**: not on Sketchfab,
  OpenGameArt or MakeHuman under CC-BY or CC0. Build them from the cornrow and
  braid pieces (`pervertex Hair braid piece`, `mh_braid01`) if wanted.
- **Men's cuts:** Quaternius `Hair_SimpleParted` (CC0, 1.3k tris) and the five
  VincentFreelance collections (about 100 realistic cuts, untextured, one scene
  per collection) cover most. No clean fade, taper, high top, quiff or
  pompadour as a single model.
- **Buzz cut:** filled by Quaternius `Hair_Buzzed` and `Hair_BuzzedFemale`
  (CC0, 830 tris each, a clean scalp shell). A `punkduck_swim_cap` (MakeHuman,
  CC-BY, 924 tris) is a second shell to start from.
- **Head coverings:** full-head hijabs now exist (witmerad Hijab 1, 2, 2.2, 3:
  head-hugging with a face opening, CC-BY), a Sikh turban (good), two wrapped
  turbans, a kufiya, a medieval head wrap that works as a bonnet, and
  MakeHuman's `elvs_charity_veil1`, `elvs_dirty_lil_maid_bonnet`, beanies and
  `culturalibre_bandana_mask`. **Still missing: a satin bonnet, a durag on a real
  head shape (two heavy sculpts exist), a gele or tichel head-tie, a wave cap.**
- **Third-pass result:** still missing after a much wider search: knotless braids, twist-out, senegalese twists, faux locs, crochet braids, silk press, satin bonnet, gele or tichel, wave cap, fade, taper, high top, quiff, pompadour. The licensed route for the braid and loc family is to build from `zacko Braid`, `vinhne3 Braid Model`, `pervertex Hair braid piece` and the MakeHuman braids, or to use the free Blender braid tools in the owner list. The owner list also has itch.io, BlendSwap, Mixamo and $0 marketplace items I cannot fetch.
- **Mohawk:** only a stylized one in the paid Quaternius Source pack. Nothing
  licensed and free; all free hits were characters.

**Second pass, 2026-11-20.** Searched about 60 more Sketchfab terms and synonyms
(dreads, crochet, silk press, taper, high top, head scarf, gele, tichel, wave cap,
kufi and others), BlenderKit (public search API), the Smithsonian open-access
models on Sketchfab, OpenGameArt headwear, GitHub, and the rest of the
MakeHuman asset packs. Sketchfab's CC-BY and CC0 pool is simply empty for
knotless braids, senegalese twists, faux locs, crochet braids, silk press, high
top fades, wave caps, satin bonnets and durags with a real head shape: the
searches returned furniture, food and game characters. The new finds are in
"Accepted: second pass" below.
